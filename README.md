# 🛡️ Official Priva Ecosystem — 100% Client-Side Privacy Suite

Welcome to the official repository and documentation hub for the **Priva Ecosystem**. We build ultra-fast, 100% client-side, zero-cloud web applications for global professionals, freelancers, students, and businesses. 

All document processing happens locally inside your web browser—your confidential files and financial records never touch an external server.

## 1. 📄 [PrivaPDF.pro](https://privapdf.pro) — Multilingual Audio PDF Reader, True PDF Editor & Converter
**Official Website:** [https://privapdf.pro](https://privapdf.pro)

**PrivaPDF Pro** is a 100% private, browser-based PDF workspace and assistive text-to-speech (TTS) audio reader designed as a lightweight, zero-cloud alternative to heavy desktop software.

### Key Capabilities:
* **15+ Language Audio Book & Document Reader:** Listen to PDF books, research papers, and legal contracts aloud with natural voice synthesis and live word-by-word highlighting (supports English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Dutch, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, and more).
* **True Word-Style PDF Text Editor:** Edit existing text directly inside your PDF, insert new paragraphs, annotate pages, and sign documents locally.
* **Confidential Data Redactor:** Permanently black out sensitive text, bank numbers, and personal identifiers before sharing files.
* **Instant PDF Utilities:** Merge PDFs, split pages, compress file size, and convert Images to PDF with zero server uploads.

## 2. 💼 [PrivaBiz.pro](https://privabiz.pro) — 6-in-1 Global Business, Invoice, QR & Barcode Suite
**Official Website:** [https://privabiz.pro](https://privabiz.pro)

**PrivaBiz Pro** is an all-in-one client-side business document, billing, and retail labeling suite built for global freelancers, retail store owners, landlords, and agencies.

### 6 Built-In Business Tools:
1. **Professional Invoice & Tax Bill Maker:** Create multi-currency (`USD $`, `GBP £`, `EUR €`, `INR ₹`, `AED`, `SGD S$`, `HKD`, `AUD A$`, `CAD C$`) tax, VAT, and GST invoices with custom company logos and instant vector PDF export.
2. **Never-Expire Custom Logo QR & Digital Business Card (vCard) Studio:** Generate 100% permanent static QR codes with center brand logos and contact-saving vCards that never deactivate or require monthly subscriptions.
3. **Bulk Retail Barcode & Price Tag Sheet Maker:** Convert Excel/CSV product lists into printable 21-sticker A4 retail price tag sheets with scannable `CODE-128` barcodes.
4. **Landlord Rent Receipt & Quick Tenancy Agreement Generator:** Generate official rent payment receipts and residential tenancy agreements with signature blocks in seconds.
5. **Ready-Made Legal Contracts & NDA Maker:** Create Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), Independent Contractor / Freelance Agreements, and Official Job Offer Letters.
6. **Executive Cover Letter & B2B Client Proposal Generator:** Draft ATS-friendly job application cover letters and high-converting client pitch proposals.
